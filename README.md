# Paytential


Watch the demo [here](https://youtu.be/H1TGQGHPOEk).

### 🌆️ Mission Statement


At Paytential, our mission is to empower individuals to master personal finance through engaging interactive simulations. By combining gamification, education, and digital technology adaptation, we create innovative learning experiences that make financial education accessible and enjoyable, helping users build essential skills for a secure financial future.

___

### 🚀 Problem Statement

Those who are first-generation, low-income, and/or BIPOC face the unique challenges of attaining financial literacy, which is essential for their personal and professional success. These individuals often lack the critical financial education and resources that provide a financial foundation for budgeting, saving, investing, and credit management. Along with systemic inequities in education, limited financial conversations in the household exacerbate this knowledge gap as opposed to those in agent groups who have been systematically advantaged by society and have the privilege of passing wealth and financial knowledge. As a result, the cycle of poverty persists as individuals need financial literacy to be able to make informed financial decisions that promote long-term economic mobility and build generational wealth.

___

### 🖋️ Features & User Stories

- **Dynamic Calendar Component (MVP)**:
  - **Purpose**: Displays monthly financial breakdowns and facilitates tracking of income, expenses, and savings.
  - **User Flow**: Users navigate through the calendar to manage monthly budgets and view transaction details.

- **Budget Setting (MVP)**:
  - **Purpose**: Enables users to define budgets for needs, wants, and savings.
  - **User Flow**: Users input their income and allocate percentages to different categories.

- **Random Events (MVP)**:
  - **Purpose**: Introduces realistic financial scenarios (e.g., unexpected expenses or windfalls).
  - **User Flow**: Users respond to events that affect their budgets, making adjustments as needed.

- **Interactive Feedback (MVP)**:
  - **Purpose**: Provides insights into spending habits and financial health.
  - **User Flow**: Users receive recommendations and summaries based on their simulation performance.

- **User-Centered Design (MVP)**:
  - **Purpose**: Focuses on intuitive navigation and responsiveness across devices.
  - **User Flow**: Users access the platform seamlessly on both desktop and mobile.

### ☀ Summary

#### According to the FINRA Foundation's 2018 National Financial Capability Study (NFCS) report:

- Over half (53%) of Americans feel anxious about their personal finances.
- Nearly half (49%) of respondents lack emergency funds, while 46% have insufficient savings for three months of expenses.
- Costly credit card practices are prevalent, affecting 42% of cardholders, particularly younger and lower-income individuals.
- Among those with student loans, nearly half regret attending a more expensive institution.

#### According to the OECD's Program for International Student Assessment (PISA) 2018:

- Significant financial literacy gaps exist between top and bottom performers in the U.S., perpetuating economic inequalities and limited mobility.

These findings highlight the urgent need for effective financial literacy tools like Paytential to address systemic gaps and foster economic equity.
